## Problem
US resellers price items by opening five tabs: eBay sold listings, Poshmark sold, Mercari sold, then a fee calculator for each platform. Every platform charges differently (eBay 13.6% + per-order fee, Poshmark 20%, Mercari 10%, Depop 3.3% + $0.45, Etsy 6.5% + processing).

## Free
- On any eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Depop or Etsy listing: one-click **sold** searches on eBay, Poshmark and Mercari for the same item.
- Profit table: what you keep on each platform at this price, after fees, shipping and your cost.
- On a sold-results page you opened: number of sold items found.

## Pro ($5.99/mo or $39 lifetime)
- Sold-page summary: median, middle-half range, low/high, outliers removed.
- "Price for my profit": the price you need on each platform to keep $X.
- Inventory log: save items with cost, target price and platform; export CSV.
- Edit fee rates (categories, boosted listings, promoted listings).

## Rules
Read-only. Never lists, relists, shares or messages. Never fetches other pages in the background: sold comps open as normal tabs the user looks at.
