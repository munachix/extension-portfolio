## Problem
Bookkeepers lose hours hunting the one transaction that makes a reconciliation off by a few dollars. The usual causes are mechanical: something cleared that shouldn't be, something not cleared that should, a sign flipped, two digits swapped, a duplicate. They can all be found by arithmetic.

## Free
- On the QBO Reconcile page: reads the difference and the transactions on screen and lists likely causes (single transaction, half-the-difference sign error, ÷9 transposition, duplicates). Suspect rows are highlighted. Never ticks or edits anything.
- Readable tab titles for QBO ("Company · Page").

## Pro ($9/mo or $79/yr)
- Combinations: 2–3 transactions that together explain the difference (check and uncheck).
- Paste mode: paste any list of amounts (from a bank statement, CSV, other software) and a difference.
- Export candidates to CSV for the client file.

## Rules
Read-only. Never clicks checkboxes, never edits or saves transactions. Works on the page the user has open. No data leaves the browser.
