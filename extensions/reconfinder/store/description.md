Reconciliation off by a few dollars? ReconFinder shows you which transaction caused it, right on the QuickBooks Online Reconcile screen.

Most reconciliation differences come from a handful of mechanical mistakes. ReconFinder checks all of them in a second:

• Not ticked yet: an uncleared transaction equal to the difference
• Ticked by mistake: a cleared transaction that shouldn't be on this statement
• Wrong sign: a payment entered as a deposit, or the reverse (half the difference)
• Digits swapped: $1,500 entered as $1,050 (differences divisible by 9)
• Entered twice: the same amount and payee cleared twice
• Missing: nothing on screen explains it, so look for this amount on the statement

Click a suggestion to jump to and highlight the transaction. ReconFinder never ticks, edits or saves anything: you stay in control.
