Stop applying to "remote" jobs you can't take. WorkFromWhere is a remote job location checker that tells you, right on the job post, whether the job accepts applicants from your country.

Many remote jobs are really "remote, US only", "EU work permit required" or "must overlap with EST hours", and the rule is often hidden at the bottom of the post. WorkFromWhere reads the post for you and shows a clear badge:

🟢 Open to you: worldwide, or your country/region is included
🔴 Restricted: with the reason, e.g. "Only open to the United States"
🟡 Unclear: no location rule found, so check the post

It highlights the exact sentence that decided the result, so you can always check it yourself.
