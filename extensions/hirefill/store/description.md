HOW IT WORKS
1. Upload your CV (PDF or Word). Check your profile.
2. Answer 10 common questions once: work authorization, sponsorship, notice period, salary, and optional demographic questions (default "Prefer not to say").
3. On any application, click "⚡ Fill with HireFill". Fields turn green (filled), yellow (please check) or red (needs you). "Next empty field" takes you to what's left.
