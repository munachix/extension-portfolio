Find out if a company sponsors work visas before you apply. VisaFlag is a visa sponsor checker that puts a badge next to the company name on LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, Reed and Totaljobs.

The official sponsor lists are public, but they're huge spreadsheets nobody checks while job hunting. VisaFlag checks them for you, right on the job post:

🇺🇸 United States: H-1B filings from the US Department of Labor
🇬🇧 United Kingdom: the Home Office register of licensed sponsors (Skilled Worker and other routes, A/B rating)
🇨🇦 Canada: employers with a positive LMIA in the last 4 quarters
🇳🇱 Netherlands: IND recognised sponsors (highly skilled migrants)

HOW IT WORKS
• ✓ Sponsor: the company is on the official list
• ≈ Likely sponsor: a close name match. We show the registered name ("Google (UK) Limited") so you can confirm it's the same company
• ✕ Not listed: not found on that country's list
• Click a badge for details: visa routes, rating, H-1B counts and the official registered name
