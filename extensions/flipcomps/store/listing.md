## Description (1315/16000)
Price your flips in seconds. FlipComps shows what an item really sells for and how much you'd keep on each marketplace, right on the listing you're looking at.

ON ANY EBAY, POSHMARK, MERCARI, DEPOP OR ETSY LISTING
• One click to SOLD listings for the same item on eBay, Poshmark and Mercari
• Profit table: fees and what you keep on eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Depop and Etsy, after your cost and shipping
• Uses current US fees: eBay 13.6% + per-order fee (category rates included), Poshmark $2.95 / 20%, Mercari 10%, Depop 3.3% + $0.45, Etsy 6.5% + processing

ON SOLD RESULTS YOU OPEN
• Free: how many sold items are on the page
• Pro: median sold price, the typical range and low/high, with outliers (lots, parts, typos) removed

PRO ($5.99/month or $39 lifetime)
• Sold-page summary
• "Price for my profit": the price you need on each platform to keep your target profit
• Inventory log with cost and target price, export to CSV
• Edit fee rates for your categories, boosted or promoted listings

SAFE FOR YOUR ACCOUNTS
FlipComps is read-only. It never lists, relists, shares, offers or messages for you, and it doesn't load pages in the background: sold searches open as normal tabs.

Not affiliated with eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Depop or Etsy. Fees can change; check each platform's fee page for your category.
