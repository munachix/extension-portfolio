Support

A real person replies, usually within one working day.

Email support@REPLACE-ME.com. Tell us which extension, what you expected and what happened. If it’s about a website, the page address helps us fix it fast.

Lost your license key?

It’s in the receipt email from Creem. Search your inbox for “Creem”. Still missing? Email us from the address you paid with and we’ll resend it.

Moving to a new computer

Install the extension and paste the same key under Settings. Removing the key on the old browser frees it up.

Something stopped working

Websites change their pages. Fields or badges that we can’t read are shown as “needs you” or “unclear” instead of guessing. Send us the page address and we’ll update the extension.
