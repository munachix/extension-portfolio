How it works

Three steps, then it just runs.

  1. Open a listing

    Yours or anyone’s, on any of the five platforms.

  2. See what you keep

    Every platform’s fees on one table, with your cost and shipping taken off.

  3. Check what it sold for

    One click to sold results; FlipComps summarises the prices on the page.

The details

Built for the way it really goes.

Real median, not wishful thinking

Lots, parts-only and typo prices are dropped before the median and typical range are worked out.

Current fees

eBay 13.6% + per-order fee with category rates, Poshmark $2.95 / 20%, Mercari, Depop and Etsy fees built in.

Price for your profit

Pro works backwards: the price you need on each platform to keep, say, $20.

Read-only

It never lists, relists, shares or messages, so it can’t put your seller account at risk.

Questions

Things people ask first.

What counts as one of the 10 daily summaries?

Each different search you open. Re-opening, scrolling or re-sorting the same search is free.

Is it allowed by eBay and Poshmark?

FlipComps only reads the page you’re already looking at. It doesn’t automate anything on your account.
