Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

WorkFromWhere does not collect, sell or share your data. No servers receive your browsing, and there is no analytics or tracking.

What stays on your computer

  • Your country and time zone
  • Your settings, custom phrase rules and saved jobs (Pro)
  • Your license status (Pro)

These are kept in your browser’s extension storage (chrome.storage.local). They are not synced to us.

What it reads

On supported job sites, and on other pages when you click “Check this page”, WorkFromWhere reads the job post you are viewing to show the badge. The text is processed on your computer.

What it sends, and to whom

WhereWhat and when
License serverOnly when you enter a Pro key: the key and a random id for this browser, so our server can ask Creem (our payment provider) if it’s valid. Re-checked about once a week.

Nothing else leaves your computer. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking code in WorkFromWhere, and we never sell or share data.

Permissions, explained

PermissionWhy it’s needed
storageKeeps your country, settings and saved jobs in your browser.
activeTab, scriptingReads one tab once, when you click “Check this page” on a site we don’t list.
Job sitesReads the job description and location on the post you open.

Payments

Purchases are handled by Creem, our merchant of record. Creem receives your payment details and email under its own privacy policy; we receive your email and order so we can support you and honour refunds.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the extension to remove everything it stored. To ask about anything we hold (your order email), write to support@REPLACE-ME.com.

Chrome Web Store user data policy

WorkFromWhere’s use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide the features described above.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we update the date above and describe the change in the extension’s release notes. Questions: support@REPLACE-ME.com.
