How it works

Three steps, then it just runs.

  1. Tell it where you are

    Your country and time zone. It’s guessed from your browser; you confirm.

  2. Open any job post

    A badge appears: green open to you, red restricted, yellow unclear.

  3. See why

    Click the badge and the exact sentence is highlighted in the post.

Built for the way it really goes.

Questions

Things people ask first.

How accurate is it?

It quotes the sentence it used, so you can always check. When a post doesn’t say, the badge is yellow rather than guessing.

Does it read my LinkedIn account?

No. It reads the job post on the page you’re viewing, on your computer, and nothing else.
