How it works

Three steps, then it just runs.

  1. Upload your CV

    PDF or Word. It’s read on your computer into a profile you can check and correct.

  2. Answer the usual questions once

    Work authorization, sponsorship, notice period, salary. Demographic questions default to “prefer not to say”.

  3. Click Fill on any application

    Green is filled, yellow is worth a look, red needs you. “Next empty field” jumps straight there.

Questions

Things people ask first.

Is autofill really unlimited on the free plan?

Yes. Autofill, the Workday handling, the Answer Bank and the tracker have no limit and no time limit. Pro is only for the AI writing help.

Will it submit an application for me?

No. HireFill refuses to click Submit, Apply or Send buttons. It fills, you review, you submit.

Where does my CV go?

Nowhere. It’s read and stored in your browser. If you choose to use your own OpenAI, Anthropic or Google key for AI help, only the text for that request goes to that provider.

What if a portal changes and a field stops filling?

It’s marked red so you see it, and you can fill it yourself. Email us the page and we’ll fix it, usually within days.
