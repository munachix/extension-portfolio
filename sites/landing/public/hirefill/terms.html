The service

HireFill is a Chrome extension made by Plainwork. Fills job applications from your CV, Workday included. You review, you submit. Free features are free to use for as long as we offer the extension.

Your applications are yours

HireFill fills forms from the profile you give it. You are responsible for checking every field before you submit, and for the truth of what you send to employers.

AI writing

AI drafts can be wrong. The truth guard flags facts that aren’t in your profile, but it can miss things. Read and edit every draft before you use it.
