Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

HireFill has no servers that receive your data, no account and no tracking. Your CV and profile stay in your browser.

What stays on your computer

  • Your profile: contact details, work history, education and skills
  • Your CV files and any tailored versions
  • Answers you save, your application tracker and settings
  • How many free AI drafts you’ve used, and your license status

These are kept in your browser’s extension storage (chrome.storage.local). They are not synced to us.

What it reads

On job application sites, HireFill reads the form you are viewing and fills it from your profile. When you ask it to tailor your CV, it reads the job post on the page. This happens on your computer.

What it sends, and to whom

WhereWhat and when
AI provider (optional)Only if you add your own OpenAI, Anthropic or Google key: the text needed for that request (relevant parts of your profile and the job post), sent to the provider you picked, under their privacy policy. Chrome’s built-in AI runs on your computer and sends nothing.
License serverOnly when you enter a Pro key: the key and a random id for this browser, so our server can ask Creem (our payment provider) if it’s valid. Re-checked about once a week.

Nothing else leaves your computer. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking code in HireFill, and we never sell or share data.

Permissions, explained

PermissionWhy it’s needed
storage, unlimitedStorageKeeps your profile and CV files in your browser. CV files can be larger than the default limit.
sidePanelOpens the HireFill app beside the application.
scripting, activeTab“Fill the current tab” on sites not listed, only when you click.
Job portalsShows the Fill button and fills the form you’re looking at, including forms inside frames (iCIMS, Taleo).
AI providers (optional)Asked for only if you choose to use your own key.

Payments

Purchases are handled by Creem, our merchant of record. Creem receives your payment details and email under its own privacy policy; we receive your email and order so we can support you and honour refunds.

Deleting your data

Click “Delete all my data” in HireFill’s settings, or uninstall the extension. Both remove everything it stored. To ask about anything we hold (your order email), write to support@REPLACE-ME.com.

Chrome Web Store user data policy

HireFill’s use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide the features described above.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we update the date above and describe the change in the extension’s release notes. Questions: support@REPLACE-ME.com.
