How we work

Three rules every tool follows.

01

The free version does the job.

If someone else gives a feature away, so do we, with no limit. Pro is for the extras that save serious time, never for the basics.

02

Your data stays with you.

CVs, client books, photos and browsing are processed in your browser. No accounts, no analytics, nothing sold. The only thing we ever see is a license key.

03

It never acts for you.

Our tools read and suggest. They don’t submit applications, list items, tick reconciliations or post anything. You stay in control of every account.

Pricing

Pay once, where we can.

Most of our tools are a single payment you’ll never hear about again. The two that need ongoing work offer a lifetime or yearly option too. Every purchase has a 14-day refund, no questions asked.
