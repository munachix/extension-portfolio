How it works

Three steps, then it just runs.

  1. Open Reconcile in QuickBooks Online

    As you normally would, for any client.

  2. ReconFinder reads the difference

    And every transaction on screen, updating as you tick.

  3. Check the rows it highlights

    Click a suggestion to jump to the row. You decide what to change.

The details

Built for the way it really goes.

The usual suspects

A transaction not ticked or ticked by mistake, a duplicate entry, a deposit entered as a payment, or digits swapped (differences divisible by 9).

Combinations

When no single transaction explains it, ReconFinder finds 2–3 that do together.

Paste mode

Pro works on any list: paste from a bank statement, spreadsheet or other software.

Readable tabs

Tab titles become “Client name · Reconcile”, so you can tell client files apart.

Questions

Things people ask first.

Can it change my client’s books?

No. ReconFinder only reads the screen and highlights rows. It never ticks, edits or saves.

Is client data sent anywhere?

No. Amounts, names and transactions are processed in your browser and never transmitted.

Does it work with QuickBooks Desktop or Xero?

Not on the page itself, but Pro’s paste mode works with a list copied from anywhere.
