Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

BorderBatch processes your photos entirely in your browser. Photos are never uploaded, and the extension has no access to any website.

What stays on your computer

  • Your last look and saved looks
  • Your license status

These are kept in your browser’s extension storage (chrome.storage.local). They are not synced to us.

What it reads

BorderBatch only reads the photos you choose to drop into it. It has no content scripts and cannot see any web page.

What it sends, and to whom

WhereWhat and when
License serverOnly when you enter a Pro key: the key and a random id for this browser, so our server can ask Creem (our payment provider) if it’s valid. Re-checked about once a week.

Nothing else leaves your computer. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking code in BorderBatch, and we never sell or share data.

Permissions, explained

PermissionWhy it’s needed
storageRemembers your looks and license status.
sidePanelThe app runs in Chrome’s side panel.

Payments

Purchases are handled by Creem, our merchant of record. Creem receives your payment details and email under its own privacy policy; we receive your email and order so we can support you and honour refunds.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the extension to remove everything it stored. To ask about anything we hold (your order email), write to support@REPLACE-ME.com.

Chrome Web Store user data policy

BorderBatch’s use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide the features described above.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we update the date above and describe the change in the extension’s release notes. Questions: support@REPLACE-ME.com.
