The service

VisaFlag is a Chrome extension made by Plainwork. Shows whether a company sponsors work visas, right next to its name on the job post. Free features are free to use for as long as we offer the extension.

Not legal or immigration advice

VisaFlag shows what official public registers say. It does not tell you whether you qualify for a visa or whether an employer will sponsor a role. Confirm with the employer and, where needed, an immigration professional.

Data accuracy

Government lists can be out of date, and company names don’t always match exactly. “Likely” matches show the registered name so you can check. We are not responsible for decisions made from the data.
