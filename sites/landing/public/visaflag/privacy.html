Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

VisaFlag does not collect, sell or share your data. It has no analytics or tracking.

What stays on your computer

  • Your chosen countries and settings
  • The downloaded sponsor lists
  • Saved companies (Pro) and your license status

These are kept in your browser’s extension storage (chrome.storage.local). They are not synced to us.

What it reads

On supported job sites, VisaFlag reads the company name on the page you are viewing and compares it with the sponsor lists stored in your browser.

What it sends, and to whom

WhereWhat and when
Sponsor listsAbout once a week VisaFlag downloads the public sponsor lists from our website. The request contains no information about you or what you browse.
License serverOnly when you enter a Pro key: the key and a random id for this browser, so our server can ask Creem (our payment provider) if it’s valid. Re-checked about once a week.

Nothing else leaves your computer. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking code in VisaFlag, and we never sell or share data.

Permissions, explained

PermissionWhy it’s needed
storageKeeps your settings, saved companies and the sponsor lists in your browser.
alarmsChecks once a week for updated lists.
Data websiteDownloads the public sponsor lists. No user data is sent.
Job sitesReads the company name on the job page you open, to show the badge.

Payments

Purchases are handled by Creem, our merchant of record. Creem receives your payment details and email under its own privacy policy; we receive your email and order so we can support you and honour refunds.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the extension to remove everything it stored. To ask about anything we hold (your order email), write to support@REPLACE-ME.com.

Chrome Web Store user data policy

VisaFlag’s use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide the features described above.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we update the date above and describe the change in the extension’s release notes. Questions: support@REPLACE-ME.com.
