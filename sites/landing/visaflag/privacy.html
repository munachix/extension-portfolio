Privacy policy: VisaFlag

Last updated: 5 November 2026

The short version

VisaFlag does not collect, sell or share your data. It has no analytics or tracking.

What happens on your computer

On supported job sites, VisaFlag reads the company name on the page you are viewing and compares it with sponsor lists stored in your browser. Your browsing and the companies you look at are never sent anywhere.

What it downloads

About once a week it downloads public sponsor lists (built from official UK, US, Canadian and Dutch government data) from our website. These downloads contain no information about you.

Stored locally

Your chosen countries, settings, saved companies (Pro) and license status are kept in your browser’s extension storage. Uninstalling deletes them.

License checks (Pro only)

When you enter a license key, the extension sends that key and a random id for this browser to our license server, which asks our payment provider (Creem) whether it is valid. It re-checks about once a week. Creem’s own privacy policy covers your purchase.
