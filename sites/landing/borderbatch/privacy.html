Privacy policy: BorderBatch

Last updated: 5 November 2026

BorderBatch processes your photos entirely in your browser. Photos are never uploaded, and the extension has no access to any website. It stores only your chosen settings and license status in your browser.

When you enter a Pro license key, the extension sends that key and a random id for this browser to our license server, which asks our payment provider (Creem) whether it is valid. No photos or other data are sent.
