Privacy policy: HireFill

Last updated: 5 November 2026

The short version

HireFill has no servers that receive your data, no account and no tracking. Your CV and profile stay in your browser.

What is stored on your computer

Your profile (contact details, work history, education, skills), your CV files, answers you save, your application tracker and settings. They are kept in your browser’s extension storage and deleted when you uninstall or click “Delete all my data”.

Application pages

On job application sites, HireFill reads the form you are viewing and fills it from your profile. It reads the job post when you ask it to tailor your CV. This happens on your computer.

AI

By default HireFill uses Chrome’s built-in AI, which runs on your computer. If you choose to add your own OpenAI, Anthropic or Google key, the text needed for that request (your profile and the job post) is sent to that provider only, under their privacy policy. You can turn AI off completely.

License checks

When you enter a license key, the extension sends that key and a random id for this browser to our license server, which asks our payment provider (Creem) whether it is valid. Creem’s privacy policy covers your purchase.
