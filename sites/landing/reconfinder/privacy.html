Privacy policy: ReconFinder

Last updated: 5 November 2026

ReconFinder reads the QuickBooks Online Reconcile screen you have open and does all calculations in your browser. No financial data, client names or transactions are sent anywhere. There are no analytics.

When you enter a Pro license key, the key and a random id for this browser are sent to our license server, which asks our payment provider (Creem) whether it is valid.
