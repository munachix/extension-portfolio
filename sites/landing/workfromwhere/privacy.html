Privacy policy: WorkFromWhere

Last updated: 5 November 2026

The short version

WorkFromWhere does not collect, sell or share your data. It has no servers that receive your browsing data, and it contains no analytics or tracking.

What stays on your computer

  • Your chosen country and time zone
  • Your settings, custom phrase rules and saved jobs (Pro)
  • Your license key and its last check result (Pro)

These are stored in your browser’s extension storage (chrome.storage.local) and never leave your device. Uninstalling the extension deletes them.

Job pages

On supported job sites, and on other pages when you click “Check this page”, the extension reads the job post you are viewing to show a badge. This text is processed on your computer and is never sent anywhere.

License checks (Pro only)

When you enter a license key, the extension sends that key to our payment provider (Lemon Squeezy) to confirm it is valid, and re-checks it about once a week. No browsing data is sent with it. Lemon Squeezy’s own privacy policy covers your purchase.
