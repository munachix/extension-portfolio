Privacy policy: FlipComps

Last updated: 5 November 2026

FlipComps reads the listing or sold-results page you are viewing on eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Depop or Etsy and does its calculations in your browser. It does not collect, send or sell your browsing data, and has no analytics.

Your cost defaults, fee settings and inventory log are stored in your browser only.

When you enter a Pro license key, the key and a random id for this browser are sent to our license server, which asks our payment provider (Creem) whether it is valid.
